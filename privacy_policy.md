# Privacy Policy for API Checker

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **API Checker** (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile application.

API Checker is a developer utility that lets you send HTTP requests (URL, method, query parameters, headers, and body) and view responses on your device.

---

## 1. Summary

- We do **not** create user accounts.
- We do **not** collect personal information on our servers.
- Request data you enter is used only to perform the HTTP calls **you** initiate.
- A small amount of preference data is stored **locally** on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Information you provide in the App

When you use API Checker, you may enter:

- Request URLs  
- HTTP methods  
- Query parameters  
- Headers (including values you choose to type, such as tokens)  
- Request body content

This information is used only to build and send the HTTP request to the destination you specify. It is processed on your device and transmitted directly to that destination over the network.

**We do not receive, store, or have access to your request URLs, headers, bodies, or responses on any of our servers.**

### 2.2 Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally using on-device preferences (for example SharedPreferences):

- Theme preference (light / dark)  
- Response panel size (split ratio)  

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded by the App to us.

### 2.3 Information we do not collect

The App does not intentionally collect:

- Name, email, or contact details  
- Contacts, photos, or files (beyond what you type into request fields)  
- Advertising identifiers for ads

---

## 3. How Network Requests Work

When you tap send:

1. The App builds an HTTP request from your inputs.  
2. The request is sent to the URL **you** provided.  
3. The response is shown in the App on your device.

The operators of the APIs or servers you call may log or process that traffic under **their** privacy policies. You are responsible for only calling endpoints you are allowed to use and for not sending sensitive data to untrusted destinations.

---

## 4. Permissions

Depending on the platform, the App may require:

- **Internet access** — required to send HTTP requests to the URLs you enter.

No other sensitive permissions are required for core functionality.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share App preference data with third parties, because that data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party servers you contact with the App may receive the request content you send; that is under your control and those parties’ policies.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Local preferences remain on your device until you clear App data, uninstall the App, or change those settings.  
- Request/response content shown in the App is held in memory for your session as needed to display results; it is not uploaded to us.

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

API Checker is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 8. Your Choices

You can:

- Stop using the App at any time  
- Clear App storage / cache in system settings to remove local preferences  
- Uninstall the App to remove it and its local data from your device
